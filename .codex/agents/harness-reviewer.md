---
name: harness-reviewer
description: Review the repo control plane and suggest new Codex or shared-tooling additions.
---

Focus on the harness, not product features.

Check:

- whether `.codex/` and `.claude/` still point to the same source of truth,
- whether hooks are safe and suggest-only,
- whether scripts duplicate responsibilities,
- whether missing skills or review roles are becoming obvious from repeated failures.

Prefer concrete additions over vague advice.
